Redesigning the notifications sheet

The sheet needed a definition before it needed a layout. Here is one, the line it draws between the sheet and the root tabs, and the design that follows from it.

The definition

The sheet is your stack's work in progress

Anything moving between "I want this" and "it's in my library" belongs in the sheet. The root tabs hold the two ends: what you might want (Search, Calendar) and what you have (Library). The bar at the bottom of the app is a one-line view of the sheet.

Tabs, in pipeline order: Requests · Downloads · Alerts · Actions. Actions stays a tab for now.

2 · The line

Requests isn't one thing. The queue passes all four tests. The archive fails all four. A root Requests tab would only make sense for someone whose whole use of Trawl is Seerr, and Trawl is built for the person running the server.

3 · Ways in

Why people open the sheet, and where it should land

4 · The design

Requests · Downloads · Alerts · Actions

Badges count what's waiting on you, and nothing else. Requests counts pending decisions. Downloads counts failing items. Alerts counts setup problems. Badges clear when the thing is dealt with, not when the sheet is opened. Unread events don't get a badge: the bar already says what's new.

6 · Decisions for you

Three calls before building

  1. What gets logged in Alerts. Proposed: every push notification, every error, and the results of long-running work (imports, actions, searches). Instant confirmations of your own taps (Copied, Saved, Deleted, Updated, Paused, Resumed) stay banners only.
  2. Approve and Decline on the row. After a tap, the row shows "Declined · Undo" for 4 seconds before the call is sent.
  3. Which tab the sheet opens on. Proposed: the tab holding whatever the bar showed. Otherwise, the tab you used last.
7 · Build scope

Where the work lands

  • NotificationTabBarAccessory.swift: a .requests case in NotificationSheetSection with its own content view. Alerts gets the new order and rows, and drops the Pending Approval and Needs Attention sections. Badges come from .badge(_:) on each tab.
  • SeerrServiceManager: pendingRequests exists. Add an "in flight" list from the processing request filter, refreshed alongside it.
  • Bar: a tab to open, chosen by the headline's priority. It replaces opensOnDownloads with a general "open on" value.
  • InAppNotificationCenter: a flag on the success path so tap confirmations skip the log.
  • Tests: request tab journeys (approve, undo, empty, Seerr not set up), Alerts ordering, badge counts, the logging rule, and the bar-to-tab mapping. Plus the TRAWL_TEST_COVERAGE_MAP.md entries.
