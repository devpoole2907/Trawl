Trawl · Series detail · 20 Nov 2026

Getting to the newest episode

You check South Park every couple of weeks for the new episode. Today that takes five taps and two long scrolls, and the season list grows by one card a year. Below: what the screen does now, measured on a 27-season test show, then five design options.

Update · built and merged

What got built

Options A, B, D and E are in iphone-duo-polish. The newest episode is now 3 taps from the Series tab, or 2 with a long-press, with no scrolling. Captured with the same 27-season stand-in show:

  • A · Up Next: missing → Search or Pick Release. Not monitored adds a Monitor button. Downloading shows progress. Ended shows with everything on disk get no card.
  • B · Episodes: season chips (orange dot for gaps) with the episodes inline, newest first. Missing, Not Aired and Not Monitored are marked. Long-press an episode to search it, pick a release or change monitoring. The season screen is still under "Season Details".
  • D · Order: Series and Movie detail share one order: actions and downloads → episodes / release dates → about → your services → bookkeeping. The services stayed full cards rather than chips.
  • E · Elsewhere: long-press in the Series list, a "Next 3 Dec" line on rows, and a "Search for Latest Episode" action in the Shortcuts app. It has no Siri phrase, because Trawl already uses Apple's maximum of ten.
  • Second pass:
    • Search Latest Episode is also on library hits in the Search tab.
    • On short screens (phone landscape, folded Duo), the poster sits beside the title on Series, Movie, Season and Episode screens, so the actions start on the first screen.
    • The Episode screen says "Airs" for an episode still to come.
    • Checked on iPad in both orientations.
  • Codex's flows, merged:
    • Calendar opens the episode itself.
    • Seerr Approve and Decline wait for the server and show an error if it fails.
    • Notifications open the screen they're about.
  • Add Series sheet (started by Codex, finished here):
    • A Monitor choice: "Future Episodes" is one tap for a long-running show, where it used to mean turning off every season's switch. "Choose Seasons" keeps the per-season switches.
    • A Series Type choice: Standard, Daily or Anime.
    • The sheet says why Add is disabled and offers Retry.
    • Nothing can be edited while an add is in flight.
    • It no longer sends monitor: none, which left the whole series unmonitored.
  • Movies list: long-press a movie for Search for Movie, or Search for Upgrade once it has a file.
  • Landscape: Up Next sits beside the title, so its Search button starts above the tab bar and Notifications bar instead of under them.
  • Fixed along the way: season counts no longer include unaired episodes. Searching or monitoring an episode now goes to that episode's own server when you have two. Single-server library rows said "Not downloaded" for everything; they now say "Available".

A · Up Next card

Do this first

A card right under the title for the newest episode that has aired. It shows the one thing to do about that episode, plus when the next one airs. The poster shrinks to sit beside the title, so the card is on the first screen without scrolling.

Data
Sonarr already sends previousAiring and nextAiring on every series. Trawl decodes both but never shows them.
Actions
Missing → Search, plus Pick Release. Not monitored → Monitor and Search, because that's the South Park case. On disk → nothing to do, or Play if Jellyfin has it.
Hidden when
The show has ended and nothing is missing, or it isn't in the library.
Cost
Small. Reuses SonarrViewModel.searchEpisode, the release sheet and the queue progress that episode rows already show.

B · One Episodes section with a season picker

Fixes the length

The 27 season cards become a strip of season chips, newest first, with that season's episodes listed right under it. It opens on the newest season. A season with something missing gets an orange dot, so you can see gaps without opening every season.

Length
Stays about the same whether a show has 2 seasons or 27.
Rows
Each episode shows on disk, missing or "airs Wed" with its own mark. Today a missing episode and an unaired one look the same: no mark at all. Swipe or long-press a row to search it without opening the episode.
Keeps
The season screen. "Search Season" and the season's subtitle search can open it, or move into this section's menu.
Cost
Medium. The episode row already exists (SonarrSeasonEpisodeRow). The work is the chip strip and choosing which season it opens on.

Smaller alternative to B

E · Without opening the show

Cheap, pairs with A

The same newest-episode action, reachable from places you already go:

  • Long-press a series in the Series list for Search Latest Episode, labelled with the episode it will search for.
  • The Series list row can say "S27 E05 missing" instead of a season count, which is what you actually came to check.
  • A Shortcuts action ("Search for the latest South Park episode"), so a fortnightly automation or Siri does it with no taps.
Cost
Small for the menu and row text. The Shortcuts action is a new AppIntent that needs the series as an entity, so it takes a little longer.

Season progress counts episodes that haven't aired

Season 27 reads "4 of 6 episodes" when five have aired and one is missing. The count includes the unaired episode, and the bar turns purple ("incomplete") for every running season. Sonarr's own statistics leave out unaired episodes.

SonarrSeriesDetailView.seasonCard: counts seasonEpisodes.count

Missing and not-yet-aired episodes look the same

Episode rows only add a mark for an episode that's on disk or downloading. A missing episode and next week's episode both show nothing. There's also no long-press or swipe to search, so every search means opening the episode.

SonarrSeasonEpisodeRow

Next and previous air dates are never shown

Both come with every series from Sonarr and are already decoded. They're what Up Next needs, and "next Wed" would also help in the Series list.

Series and Movie detail have drifted apart

The cards come in a different order: Seerr comes before Jellyfin on Series but after it on Movies, and import issues sit near the top on Series but after Subtitles on Movies. Series also hides its stats until the show is in the library, while Movies always shows them. Choosing one order (option D) and sharing it would stop this.

cardsSection in SonarrSeriesDetailView and RadarrMovieDetailView

Four copies of the blurred-artwork background

The season screen builds its own blurred poster background instead of using the one in ArrItemDetailView. Two other files carry copies too. They match today but can drift, like the search buttons did.

SonarrSeriesSearchViews, RadarrMovieSearchViews, ArrReleaseActionContent, ArrItemDetailView

The Add sheets are built differently

Adding a movie uses the shared Quality Profile and Root Folder pickers and offers a Monitor choice. Adding a series uses its own copies of both pickers and has no Monitor choice (all, future only, latest season…) and no series type (anime, daily). For a long-running show, "future episodes only" is usually what you want, and today that means turning off 27 season switches.

SonarrAddToLibrarySheet vs RadarrAddToLibrarySheet

Release picker: consistent

Series, season, episode and movie release searches all use the same screen (ArrReleaseActionContent). Nothing to fix.
